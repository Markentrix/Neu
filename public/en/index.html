How it works

From the first call to settlement in four steps

You take care of what matters. I handle documentation and assessment and support you in dealing with the insurer, the garage or your lawyer.

  1. Get in touch

    Call or write on WhatsApp. We briefly clarify what happened and what you need now.

  2. Inspection

    Appointment by arrangement. All damage is recorded in writing and with photos, including previous and old damage.

  3. Report

    Repair costs, diminished value, repair time and, for a total loss, residual and replacement value. Prompt and court-proof.

  4. Settlement

    You submit the report to the insurer and decide on a sound basis: repair, settle on the basis of the report (fiktive Abrechnung) or sell.

Expert report instead of repair estimate

Why a repair estimate is not enough after an accident

Insurers like to accept a repair estimate with photos and claim that you would have to pay for an expert yourself. In a liability case the law says otherwise.

No cost to you

As the injured party you may choose the expert freely. The costs are usually paid by the liability insurer of the person at fault.

Financial security

Besides the repair costs, the report covers diminished value, repair time and downtime. Only then are your claims fully quantified.

Evidence

If the other party's insurer denies responsibility, a report keeps you on the safe side. In a dispute it is recognised in court.

Frequently asked questions

What accident victims ask most often

Who pays for the report?

After a not-at-fault accident the costs of the report count as part of the damage. As a rule the liability insurer of the person at fault pays them. For an at-fault accident, a valuation report or an evidence report, you commission me directly and receive an individual quote in advance.

Do I have to use the expert the insurer suggests?

No. As the injured party you have the right to commission an independent expert of your choice. An expert appointed by the insurer works on the insurer's behalf.

How quickly will I get my report?

I arrange the inspection at short notice. The report is prepared promptly so that the wait until the repair or a replacement vehicle is as short as possible.

Can I have the damage paid out without repairing?

Yes, this is called notional settlement (fiktive Abrechnung). It is common for minor damage, older vehicles or if you repair the car yourself. The report quantifies not only the repair costs but also the diminished value, which matters when you later sell the car.

What happens in the case of a total loss?

Then I determine the residual value and the replacement value and, if you wish, obtain offers for the damaged vehicle. On that basis you decide whether a repair is worthwhile or whether selling is better.

Which vehicles do you prepare reports for?

Cars, lorries and commercial vehicles, motorcycles, classic cars and electric bicycles.
