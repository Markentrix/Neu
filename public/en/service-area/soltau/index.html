How an appointment works

  1. You call or write to me and briefly describe what it is about.
  2. We settle where and when I can inspect your vehicle in or around Soltau.
  3. I document the damage or condition and prepare the report promptly.
  4. You receive the report and decide on the next steps.

After an accident in Soltau

First secure the accident scene. If anyone is injured, call 112; for an accident the police are to attend, call 110. Then you can reach me by phone. The accident checklist takes you through the first steps.
