Services

Vehicle reports in Celle: all services

From assessing the damage after an accident to valuing your classic car. I prepare every report neutrally, transparently and promptly.

By Patric Schneider, Vehicle engineering expert, Master motor vehicle technicianUpdated: October 2026

Six reports, one point of contact

  • Accident damage

    Liability damage report

    After a not-at-fault accident: all damage documented, including repair time and diminished value. For a total loss with residual and replacement value. The costs are usually paid by the other party's insurer.

    Learn more →
  • Used cars and workshops

    Evidence report

    Does the car pull to the left, or are the panel gaps uneven? Hidden previous damage and faulty repairs are recorded neutrally so you are covered against the seller or workshop.

    Learn more →
  • End of lease

    Lease return report

    Normal signs of use are not damage. An independent report protects you against excessive claims when you return a leased vehicle.

    Learn more →
  • Buying, selling, classic cars

    Valuation report

    The real market value of your vehicle, objectively documented. Special equipment as well as previous and old damage are recorded. Ideal when buying or selling, or for your classic car.

    Learn more →
  • Total loss

    Residual value assessment

    What is the unrepaired vehicle still worth? We determine the residual value regionally or Europe-wide, obtain offers and you decide without risk: repair or sell.

    Learn more →
  • Legal dispute

    Accident reconstruction report

    When the course of events is disputed: technical reconstruction of the accident as a solid basis for lawyers, insurers and courts.

    Learn more →

Which report do I need?

Match your situation. If you are unsure, call. We will clear it up in a few minutes.

Your situationSuitable report
Not-at-fault accident, damage to your vehicleLiability damage report
Total loss, the vehicle is to be soldResidual value assessment
Used car or repair is defectiveEvidence report
Return of a leased vehicle, dispute about diminished valueLease return report
Buying, selling or valuing a classic carValuation report
How the accident happened is disputed, a lawsuit is likelyAccident reconstruction report
