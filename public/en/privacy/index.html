Privacy policy

Note for the operator: this statement describes the technology of this website (contact form with photos on a Hetzner server, no cookies, no analytics tools, self-hosted fonts). The places marked in yellow must be clarified and filled in before going live: server location and data processing agreement with Hetzner, mailbox provider, retention period of the server logs. If cookies, statistics or external content are added later, the notice and this statement must be adjusted. This text does not replace a legal review. This English version is a courtesy translation; the German version is legally binding. Delete this note afterwards.

2. Overview

This website does not set cookies and does not use any tracking or analytics services. No third-party content is embedded and no fonts are loaded from external servers. We process personal data when you use the contact form or contact us, and in the form of technically necessary connection data when you visit the site.

3. Hosting and server log files

This website is operated on a server of Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany. Enter the location of the data centre and conclude or confirm the data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR with Hetzner

When you visit the website, the web server automatically processes information transmitted by your browser: shortened IP address, date and time, page requested, amount of data transferred, browser type and operating system. This data serves the security and stability of the website and is deleted after enter period, 7 days recommended. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the secure operation of the site).

4. Contact form and damage photos

If you use the contact form, we transmit your details to our server in encrypted form (HTTPS): name, email address, topic, message and, if you wish, your phone number and up to five photos. Name, email address, topic and message are required so that we can answer your enquiry.

Photos

Photos are already reduced in size in your browser. The server reduces them again and removes metadata such as the location where the photo was taken. Please do not upload photos in which people can be recognised.

Forwarding and deletion

The server forwards your enquiry with the photos by email to our mailbox. The transmission to the mail server is encrypted. Mailbox provider: enter name and address of the provider, check data processing agreement After successful delivery, the enquiry and photos are deleted from the server. If delivery fails for technical reasons, the enquiry is kept safe for 14 days at most so that it is not lost, and is then deleted.

Protection against abuse

To prevent the form from being abused by automated programs, we limit the number of enquiries per connection. For this we do not store your IP address in plain text but only a hash with a secret key, which is deleted after two days at most. We also check a field that is invisible to people and a time-limited form token. Both work without cookies and without third-party services.

Log

The form log contains only the time, reference number, number of photos and status, but no names or contact details. It is deleted after enter period, 90 days recommended.

Legal basis

Processing takes place to answer your enquiry and to prepare a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and to protect against abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Enquiry data is deleted as soon as it is no longer needed for processing and no statutory retention obligations apply.

5. Email, phone and WhatsApp

If you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, we process the data you send to handle your enquiry and for any follow-up questions. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering enquiries).

With WhatsApp, data is transmitted to WhatsApp Ireland Limited (Meta), including your phone number and metadata of the communication. Content is end-to-end encrypted. Only use WhatsApp if you agree to this. Alternatively you can reach us at any time by phone, email or through the contact form.

6. Commissions and reports

If you commission a report, we process the data required for it: your contact details, vehicle data (registration number, vehicle identification number, mileage), details of the damage event, photos of the vehicle and, for liability damage, data of the other party and their insurer. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

For settlement we pass the report on to the recipients you name, such as the insurer handling the claim, your garage or your lawyer. For calculation and residual value assessment we use industry-standard specialist systems. Enter the systems and residual value exchanges used or remove sentence

We keep reports and related documents in line with commercial and tax law requirements (generally 10 years, § 147 AO, § 257 HGB).

7. Cookies and local storage

This website does not set cookies. It uses neither statistics nor advertising tools and does not load content, maps, videos or fonts from third-party servers.

The accident checklist remembers ticked items in your browser's local storage. This information does not leave your device, is not transmitted to us and can be deleted at any time via "Reset list" or the browser settings. This is purely functional storage that is necessary for the service you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). A consent prompt and a cookie banner are therefore not required.

8. Your rights

You have the following rights with regard to your personal data:

  • Access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Objection to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21 GDPR)

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The competent authority is the State Commissioner for Data Protection of Lower Saxony (Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen), Prinzenstraße 5, 30159 Hanover, Germany.

9. Data security

The website is delivered encrypted via HTTPS. The configuration of the form is not in the publicly accessible area of the server. We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, manipulation and unauthorised access.

10. Changes

We will amend this privacy policy if the website or the legal requirements change. The version published here at the time applies.
