Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Can I book Bar Zeitreise for a private party?

Yes. By arrangement, Zeitreise can be booked exclusively for private parties, for example birthdays, company events or theme parties. Enquiries are possible by phone at +49 176 10383288 or via Instagram.

How does a party enquiry work?

Call us at +49 176 10383288 or write to us on Instagram or Facebook. We discuss date, occasion and wishes with you personally. There is no enquiry form on the website.

What music does Zeitreise play at a party?

Zeitreise plays music from the 70s, 80s and 90s: disco beats of the 70s, synth-pop of the 80s and grunge and Eurodance hits of the 90s.

What is the minimum age for Bar Zeitreise?

Bar Zeitreise is open to guests aged 18 and over.

Where is Bar Zeitreise?

Bar Zeitreise is located at Hehlentorstraße 16, 29221 Celle (Lower Saxony, Germany). The nearest bus stop is Schloßplatz. Plan your route in Google Maps.
